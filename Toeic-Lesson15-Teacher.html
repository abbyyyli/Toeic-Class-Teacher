Restaurant Tasty 介紹

Tasty is a fabulously stylish restaurant. Tasty combines a fascinating location with an elaborate restaurant and hotel concept all in the center of Garden City. The restaurant Tasty is stunningly designed with exposed brickwork, low arched ceilings and modern furnishings offering a cozy yet spacious feel. There is also a leafy inner courtyard providing a tranquil setting for al fresco dining. The menu at restaurant Tasty is both innovative and interesting combining Asian and European elements to create a totally new culinary experience. Favorites include grilled lobster, beef steak and chicken. The sushi counter is currently proving to be one of the highlights of restaurant Tasty and foodies in Garden City are flocking here. The set menus for business lunches are a steal at just 8 USD from Monday to Friday where 3 courses of amazing cuisine can be enjoyed. The wine list deserves a mention if not for the huge selection of champagnes available.
