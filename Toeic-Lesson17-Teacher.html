本堂目標

課本把主動詞一致當成「看主詞單複數，選 is 或 are」。學生大致都會，但 TOEIC 真正考的是以下四件事，本堂的深度與廣度就放在這裡：

  1. 找真正的主詞：主詞後面常接介系詞片語、分詞片語、關係子句、插入語（as well as、along with），離動詞很遠。TOEIC 會故意讓空格前面那個名詞跟主詞的單複數相反。
  2. 判斷「看起來」和「實際上」的單複數：news、economics、the United States 字尾有 -s 卻是單數；people、police、personnel 沒有 -s 卻是複數；two hundred dollars 看起來複數，其實是一筆金額。
  3. 分辨三套規則：
  4. 主詞決定：大部分句子。
  5. 靠近原則：or、nor、either…or、neither…or、not only…but also、not A but B、There is/are。

  6. of 後面的名詞決定：most of、half of、some of、percent of、the rest of 等部分詞。

  7. 把一致性跟其他文法混在一起判斷：時態、主被動、詞性、助動詞、假設語氣（suggest that + 原形）、代名詞 its/their、關係子句。TOEIC 的選項通常同時改了單複數、時態和語態，學生要能一次排除。

學生最常見的錯誤，是把離空格最近的名詞當主詞。本堂每一題都先問：「真正的主詞是哪個字？」

核心規則

主詞的單複數決定動詞形式，但不是每個動詞形式都會變。只有下面四種情況需要一致：

需要一致的位置 單數主詞（he/she/it/單數名詞） 複數主詞（we/you/they/複數名詞）
現在簡單式一般動詞 works / goes / studies work / go / study
be 動詞（現在／過去） is / was are / were
have（現在完成式、have 當動詞） has have
助動詞 do（否定、疑問） does / doesn't do / don't

不需要一致的情況（TOEIC 常用來設陷阱）：

形式 例子 說明
過去簡單式（be 除外） He worked / They worked 一般動詞過去式不分單複數
情態助動詞＋原形 He can go / They can go can、will、should、must 後面一律原形，不加 s
過去完成式 He had left / They had left had 不分單複數
未來式 He will go / They will go will 不分單複數
假設語氣 suggest that he submit 原形動詞，不加 s（見小節 10）
規則表：be / have 人稱變化（課本 p.134）
人稱 單複數 be 現在 be 過去 have 現在 have 過去
第一人稱 單數 I am I was I have I had
第一人稱 複數 we are we were we have we had
第二人稱 單數／複數 you are you were you have you had
第三人稱 單數 he / she / it is was has had
第三人稱 複數 they are were have had

易錯：you 就算只指一個人，也用 are／were。不說 you was。

現在式第三人稱單數 -s 的拼字規則
字尾 規則 例子
一般 加 -s work → works, need → needs
-s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o 加 -es pass → passes, finish → finishes, watch → watches, fix → fixes, go → goes
子音＋y 去 y 加 -ies study → studies, apply → applies
母音＋y 直接加 -s pay → pays, enjoy → enjoys
不規則 特別記 have → has, be → is, do → does
例句
  • The meeting begins at 9 sharp. 會議九點整開始。
  • My uncle lives in New York. 我叔叔住在紐約。
  • She has been working in the company for 2 years. 她已經在這家公司工作兩年了。
  • The manager doesn't approve overtime on weekends. 經理不核准週末加班。
  • The shipment was delayed, but the orders were delivered on time. 那批貨延誤了，但訂單準時送達。
  • The CEO will announce the results tomorrow. 執行長明天會公布結果。（will 後面原形，不需要一致）
TOEIC 常見用法
  • 選項同時有 is / are / be / being，或 has / have / having：先刪掉 be、being、having 這類「不是完整動詞」的選項，剩下兩個再看主詞。
  • 選項同時有 submit / submits / submitted / submitting：先看時間線索（yesterday 要過去式），再看主詞單複數。

2找真正的主詞：刪掉修飾語與插入語｜課本要點六擴充⏱ 約 14 分鐘
核心規則

動詞只跟「核心主詞」一致，不跟主詞後面的修飾語一致。TOEIC Part 5 最常考的就是這種「主詞很長」的句子。

三步找主詞法：

  1. 從句首往後找第一個「沒有被介系詞帶著」的名詞。
  2. 把下面這些修飾語全部括起來刪掉：介系詞片語、分詞片語、關係子句、同位語、插入語。
  3. 用剩下的核心主詞判斷單複數。
修飾語類型 例子（括號內刪掉） 核心主詞 動詞
介系詞片語 The list (of approved suppliers) ___ list is
分詞片語 The documents (attached to this e-mail) ___ documents are
關係子句 The employee (who trained the new interns) ___ employee is
同位語 Taipei 101, (one of the world's famous skyscrapers), ___ Taipei 101 is
插入語 Mr. Jones, (together with his secretary), ___ Mr. Jones is
插入語：與「前者」一致（課本要點六）

下面這些片語不會讓主詞變成複數，動詞只跟 A（前面的主詞）一致：

片語 中文 例句（A 是單數）
A with B A 連同 B Mr. Jones with his secretary is going to attend the meeting.
A, together with B, A 連同 B Ms. Smith, together with her subordinates, is flying to Chicago.
A, along with B, A 和 B Jack, along with his two brothers, is going to the concert.
A, accompanied by B, A 由 B 陪同 The CEO, accompanied by his assistant, is going to attend the ceremony.
A, including B, A 包括 B The house, including the basement, is seriously damaged.
A as well as B A 以及 B My boss as well as other specialists is joining the party.
A besides B 除了 B 之外還有 A The teacher besides other children is singing a song.
A, in addition to B, 除了 B 之外，A The manager, in addition to two interns, is on the trip.
A, rather than B, 是 A 而不是 B The CEO, rather than the managers, makes the final decision.
A, like B, A 和 B 一樣 Our branch, like many others, is cutting costs.

對照：A 是複數時，動詞就用複數：

  • My parents, accompanied by my brother, are traveling to Japan. 我爸媽在我哥哥陪同下要去日本旅遊。
  • My two brothers as well as my younger sister are traveling to China. 我兩個哥哥和妹妹要去中國旅遊。
易混淆比較：A and B ≠ A with B
句子 主詞 動詞 原因
The manager and his assistant are here. manager + assistant are and 連接兩個主詞 → 複數
The manager with his assistant is here. manager is with B 是修飾語 → 看 A
The manager, as well as his assistant, is here. manager is as well as B 是插入語 → 看 A

教學提示：插入語前後常有逗號。看到逗號夾起來的片語，先把它括掉。

TOEIC 常見用法
  • The cost / price / quality / number / list / series / range / variety of + 複數名詞 + 單數動詞：核心主詞是 of 前面的名詞。
  • Applications / Documents / Requests + for / from / submitted… + 複數動詞。
  • Part 5 經常把一個單數名詞放在空格正前方，讓學生誤判。例如 The reports from the regional office ___ → are。

3單一事件主詞：to V、V-ing、名詞子句｜課本要點二⏱ 約 8 分鐘
核心規則

當主詞是「一件事」而不是「一樣東西」時，動詞一律用單數。

主詞形式 結構 例句
不定詞 To + V… To train students to solve problems is an important part of college education.
動名詞 V-ing… Dealing with difficult customers is an energy-consuming task.
that 子句 That + S + V… That the picnic has been cancelled disappoints many children.
whether 子句 Whether + S + V… Whether Linda will be promoted (or not) is still undecided.
wh- 子句 How / Why / When / Where + S + V… How other people judge you is not that important.
what 子句 What + (S) + V… What we need is more time.
易混淆與例外
  1. 動名詞後面接複數名詞也不影響：
  2. Mastering two foreign languages at the same time is a difficult job.（主詞是 Mastering 這件事，不是 languages）

  3. Reading novels is my favorite pastime.

  4. 兩件事用 and 連接 → 複數：

  5. Hiking and swimming are my hobbies. 健行和游泳是我的興趣。（課本要點四）

  6. 但如果兩者是同一件事的整體，可用單數：Eating and drinking in the lab is prohibited.（一個整體規定。較進階，TOEIC 少考）

  7. what 子句後面接複數補語：What we need is more staff. 正式英文用 is，學生常因 staff/books 誤選 are。A2–B1 程度教「what 子句＝一件事＝單數」即可。

  8. V-ing 當主詞 ≠ V-ing 當形容詞：
  9. Training new staff takes time.（訓練新員工這件事 → 單數）
  10. Training sessions are held every Monday.（training 修飾 sessions，主詞是 sessions → 複數）
例句
  • To see is to believe. 眼見為憑。
  • Smoking is not allowed in this area. 這裡禁止吸菸。
  • Studying in the U.S. is one of my long-term goals. 去美國留學是我的長期目標之一。
  • Why the shipment was delayed remains unclear. 貨物為什麼延誤仍然不清楚。
  • What the customers want is faster delivery. 顧客想要的是更快的送貨。
  • Updating the software regularly helps prevent security problems. 定期更新軟體有助於避免資安問題。
TOEIC 常見用法
  • 主詞開頭是 Hiring / Expanding / Reducing / Improving / Reviewing…，空格常考 is / has / helps / requires。
  • Whether… 和 How… 開頭的主詞較少見，但出現時幾乎都是單數。

核心規則

在 There is / There are 句型中，There 不是主詞。真正的主詞在動詞後面。

句型 真正主詞 動詞
There is a manager in the conference room. a manager is
There are ten managers in the conference room. ten managers are
There is something on the desk. something is
There are two books on the desk. two books are
兩個以上主詞：靠近原則（課本 p.138）

There is/are 後面有兩個以上主詞時，課本規則是跟最靠近動詞的那個一致：

句子 判斷
There is a boy and a girl in the classroom. ✓（a boy 靠近 → is）
There are a boy and a girl in the classroom. ✗（依課本規則）
There are two nurses and a doctor in the clinic. ✓（two nurses 靠近 → are）
There is two nurses and a doctor in the clinic. ✗

老師補充：母語人士的正式寫作中也會看到 There are a boy and a girl。但 TOEIC 與課本都依「靠近原則」判斷，考試時照課本規則答。

句型 例句 真正主詞 中文

用下列連接詞連接兩個主詞時，動詞跟最靠近動詞的那個主詞一致：

連接詞 跟誰一致 例句
A or B B One or two days are enough to see Taipei City.
either A or B B Either Mr. Jones or his team members are going to assist us.

疑問句：動詞在主詞前面時，跟最靠近的 A 一致：

  • Are you or he to blame for this mistake? 是你還是他該為這個錯誤負責？

neither A nor B 看 B Neither the manager nor his assistants are on vacation.

9關係子句裡的一致：先行詞決定動詞｜新增⏱ 約 8 分鐘
核心規則

關係代名詞 who / which / that 本身沒有單複數，它代替前面的名詞（先行詞）。所以關係子句的動詞跟先行詞一致。

句子 先行詞 子句動詞
The employee who works in Room 5 is new. employee works
The employees who work in Room 5 are new. employees work
The report that was sent yesterday has errors. report was
The files which were deleted can't be recovered. files were
TOEIC 進階陷阱：one of the … who
結構 先行詞 子句動詞 例句
one of the + 複數 + who 複數名詞 複數 Ms. Lee is one of the consultants who have worked with us.
the only one of the + 複數 + who one 單數 Ms. Lee is the only one of the consultants who has worked with us.

判斷方法：問自己「who 指的是哪一群人？」

  • 句 1：在那群「跟我們合作過的顧問」當中，Lee 是其中一位 → who 指 consultants → have。
  • 句 2：在所有顧問中，只有 Lee 一人合作過 → who 指 the only one → has。

注意兩個動詞分開判斷：Ms. Lee is one of the consultants who have…。主要子句的 is 跟 Ms. Lee 一致，子句的 have 跟 consultants 一致。

例句
  • Customers who pay by credit card receive a 5% discount. 用信用卡付款的顧客可享九五折。
  • The manager who interviews new staff is on leave this week. 負責面試新員工的經理這週休假。
  • We need a printer that prints in color. 我們需要能印彩色的印表機。
  • This is one of the products that sell best in summer. 這是夏天賣得最好的產品之一。

最重要的五個陷阱
  1. 把最靠近空格的名詞當主詞 → 先刪介系詞片語、分詞片語、關係子句、插入語。
  2. the number of 和 a number of 搞反 → The number 是數字 → 單數；A number 是很多 → 複數。
  3. as well as 和 not only…but also 搞反 → as well as 看前面；not only…but also 看後面。
  4. 字尾 -s 的單數名詞（news、economics、the United States、headquarters）和沒有 -s 的複數名詞（people、police、personnel）。
  5. 以為單數主詞一定要加 s → suggest that + 原形、助動詞後面、過去式都不加。
