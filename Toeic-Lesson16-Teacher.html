Restaurant Tasty 介紹

Tasty is a fabulously stylish restaurant. Tasty combines a fascinating location with an elaborate restaurant and hotel concept all in the center of Garden City. The restaurant Tasty is stunningly designed with exposed brickwork, low arched ceilings and modern furnishings offering a cozy yet spacious feel. There is also a leafy inner courtyard providing a tranquil setting for al fresco dining. The menu at restaurant Tasty is both innovative and interesting combining Asian and European elements to create a totally new culinary experience. Favorites include grilled lobster, beef steak and chicken. The sushi counter is currently proving to be one of the highlights of restaurant Tasty and foodies in Garden City are flocking here. The set menus for business lunches are a steal at just 8 USD from Monday to Friday where 3 courses of amazing cuisine can be enjoyed. The wine list deserves a mention if not for the huge selection of champagnes available.

What does the first article suggest about the restaurant?

詳細解題流程

fabulously stylish（極為時尚）、stunningly designed（設計令人驚豔）、modern furnishings（現代家具）都在描寫時髦風格，C 的 trendy 是同義改寫。

C · It really is a trendy restaurant.對。stylish、stunningly designed、modern 三處證據共同支持 trendy。
D · It is located in a rural area.錯。原文 all in the center of Garden City，位於市中心，不是鄉村。

182語境字義⭐ 重點題
In the first article, the word “tranquil” in paragraph 1 line 4, is closest in meaning to
第一篇文章第一段第四行的 tranquil，意思最接近哪一個？
Awonderful很棒的；令人愉快的
Btemperate（氣候）溫和的；有節制的
Ccritical批判的；關鍵的
Dnegative負面的
正解：A · wonderful
詳細解題流程

定位 a leafy inner courtyard providing a tranquil setting for al fresco dining → tranquil 原意是「寧靜的」，在這裡描寫令人舒服的用餐環境，是正面形容詞 → 四個選項沒有 peaceful／calm，只有 wonderful 是同方向的正面描述 → 選 A。

為什麼正解對

tranquil 的核心意思是「寧靜、平和」。本題四個選項都沒有 peaceful，只能選語意方向相同的正面字；綠意盎然的中庭、戶外用餐、寧靜的環境合起來是「宜人、很棒」的感覺，所以選 wonderful。

四個選項分析
選項為什麼對／錯例句
A · wonderful對。原文是正面的環境描寫，wonderful 是四項中唯一方向一致的正面形容詞。We had a wonderful time at the beach.我們在海邊度過了很愉快的時光。
B · temperate錯。temperate 多指氣候溫和或人有節制，原文沒有談天氣，不能從戶外用餐自行推出氣候。The island has a temperate climate all year.這座島嶼全年氣候溫和。
C · critical錯。critical 是「批判的、關鍵的」，與寧靜的環境無關。The manager was critical of the new plan.經理對新計畫持批評態度。
D · negative錯。negative 是負面的，和原文的正面描述相反。The movie received negative reviews.這部電影得到負面評價。
重要單字（詞性／中文／搭配／例句）
  • tranquil（adj. 寧靜的；a tranquil setting；The garden is quiet and tranquil.）
  • peaceful / calm（adj. 平靜的，tranquil 的常見同義字；a peaceful village；We stayed in a peaceful village.）
重要搭配（意思／句型／例句）
  • a tranquil setting for + N.＝適合……的寧靜環境；The park is a tranquil setting for a picnic.
  • al fresco dining＝戶外用餐；The café offers al fresco dining in summer.

184評論主旨與暗示⭐ 重點題
What does the reviewer imply about the restaurant?
評論者對這家餐廳暗示了什麼？
AThe food there tasted terrible.那裡的食物很難吃。
BThe service quality was extremely high.服務品質非常高。
CThe restaurant atmosphere was excellent.餐廳的氣氛極佳。
DThe staff members were not so friendly.員工不太友善。
正解：D · The staff members were not so friendly.
詳細解題流程

評論題先看標題「Good food, not very friendly staff」→ 正文 What let the place down was the service. The staff just didn’t come across in a very friendly way → 評論的主要訊息是員工不友善 → 選 D。

為什麼正解對

標題直接寫 not very friendly staff，正文又說 staff didn’t come across in a very friendly way，D 是同義改寫，也是整篇評論的重點。

四個選項分析
選項為什麼對／錯
A · The food there tasted terrible.錯。原文 the food was really good、Food: Excellent，食物很好。
B · The service quality was extremely high.錯。原文 Service: Fair，還抱怨沒人來確認餐點，服務不是極佳。
C · The restaurant atmosphere was excellent.錯。Atmosphere: Excellent 確實出現在評分欄，但題目問 imply（暗示）的評論重點；標題與正文都集中在員工不友善，這是 D 而不是 C。
D · The staff members were not so friendly.對。標題 not very friendly staff＋正文 didn’t come across in a very friendly way。
重要單字（詞性／中文／搭配／例句）
  • imply（v. 暗示；What does the writer imply?；His smile implied that he agreed.）
  • friendly（adj. 友善的；friendly staff；The staff were friendly and helpful.）
重要搭配（意思／句型／例句）
  • let + 人／地方 + down＝使……失望／扣分；Poor service let the restaurant down.
  • come across as + adj.＝給人……的印象；She came across as very confident.
